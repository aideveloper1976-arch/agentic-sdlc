## Task: Competitor Scan

### Input
Product idea, market, or feature area

### Steps
1. Search for likely competitors or substitutes.
2. Open the most relevant product pages.
3. Verify capabilities, positioning, and pricing from real sources.
4. Compare patterns and identify gaps.
5. Return evidence-backed findings.

---

## Task: Pricing and Packaging Research

### Input
Target product category or competitor list

### Steps
1. Locate official pricing or plan pages.
2. Capture plan tiers, limits, and packaging signals.
3. Note unclear or unavailable pricing explicitly.
4. Summarize pricing patterns and outliers.

---

## Task: UX Pattern Research

### Input
Target flow such as onboarding, checkout, or setup

### Steps
1. Search for relevant products and flow examples.
2. Open product or help pages that reveal the flow.
3. Capture repeated interaction patterns.
4. Distinguish expected best practices from direct evidence.
