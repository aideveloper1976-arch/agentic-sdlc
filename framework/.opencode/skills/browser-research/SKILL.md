# Browser Research

## Goal
Produce source-backed research for product discovery, competitor analysis, market trends, pricing, UX patterns, and feature expectations.

## System of Record
- Jira project: `$JIRA_PROJECT_KEY` for workflow linkage.
- Confluence space: `PROD` for research artefacts.

## When to Use
- During idea validation and product discovery.
- When comparing competitor capabilities or pricing.
- When gathering external evidence for requirements, UX, or positioning.

## Required Inputs
- Research topic or question.
- Target market, user segment, or competitor set if known.
- Any constraints on geography, industry, or product category.

## Workflow
1. Search using `browser_research` tools.
2. Open real pages before making claims.
3. Capture at least three credible sources where feasible.
4. Separate direct evidence from interpretation.
5. Save evidence when the findings inform backlog, design, or release decisions.
6. Update linked Jira item in the configured Jira project and write checkpoint in `docs/product/traceability/checkpoints`.

## Required Output
## Research Summary
## Target Users
## Competitors Found
## Feature Patterns
## Pricing / Packaging Notes
## UX / Flow Patterns
## Opportunities / Gaps
## Candidate USP
## Source Evidence

## Rules
- Never rely only on search snippets.
- Do not invent competitor capabilities or pricing.
- Prefer official product pages, documentation, pricing pages, and reputable analysis.
- Mark uncertainty clearly when evidence is partial.
